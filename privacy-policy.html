Privacy Policy

We built Hireon to help you find real online courses and certification programs, along with legitimate gift card rewards, without surprises. Here's exactly what information we collect, why, and how it's protected — in plain English.

This Privacy Policy explains how Hireon ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website and browse our two types of listings: online courses and certification programs and gift card reward offers. We know legal pages can feel intimidating — so we've kept this one short, honest, and written for humans, not lawyers.

By using Hireon, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you don't agree, we understand — you're always free not to use the site.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. If you choose to enroll in a course listing or claim a gift card reward, you'll be taken to that program's or partner's own page to complete the process. We do not collect your name, date of birth, or contact details on this site unless you explicitly submit them to us directly — for example, through a contact form or email.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we automatically receive some technical information when you visit, including:

  • Browser type and device type
  • General location (city/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Pages viewed, listings clicked, and time spent on the site
  • Referring website (how you found us)

This information helps us understand which course listings and gift card offers are useful to visitors and keep the site running smoothly — it does not identify you personally.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To display and improve the relevance of course listings and gift card offers
  • To understand site traffic and fix technical issues
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse across both listing types
  • To respond to messages you send us directly

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers, and we never share information with third parties for their own unrelated marketing purposes.

3. CPA & Affiliate Disclosure

Hireon operates as a listings and marketing platform. This means the "Enroll Now" and "Redeem Now" buttons on our site link out to third-party course providers, certification bodies, retailers, or offer providers. We may receive compensation when you click through or complete an action on a partner's page — whether that's enrolling in a program or claiming a gift card reward. This is how we keep the site free to use.

This compensation never affects how we describe a listing or reward, and it costs you nothing extra to enroll or redeem. We simply believe you deserve to know how the site is funded — full transparency, no hidden catch.

4. Gift Card Reward Offers

Our gift card listings connect you with third-party reward and incentive programs. Some offers may require completing a short set of steps (such as a survey, sign-up, or trial) to qualify — this is always disclosed on the individual offer before you begin. Hireon does not issue, fund, or guarantee gift cards directly; fulfillment is handled by the third-party partner named on each listing.

We review partners before listing their offers, but we recommend reading a partner's own terms before providing any information to qualify for a reward.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use small amounts of local storage and standard analytics cookies to remember basic preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time — the site will still function, though some features may be less personalized.

6. Third-Party Links & Offers

Once you click "Enroll Now" or "Redeem Now," you leave Hireon and land on a partner's website, which has its own privacy practices. We carefully review the partners we list, but we are not responsible for the privacy policies or content of external sites. We recommend reviewing a partner's own privacy policy before submitting any personal information to them.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including HTTPS encryption, to protect the limited information we do collect. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to keep your experience safe.

8. Data Retention

We retain automatically collected technical data only for as long as needed to understand site usage and improve our listings, and we delete or anonymize it on a routine basis. Any information you send us directly (for example, via email) is kept only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your request.

9. Your Privacy Rights

If you're a US resident, depending on your state you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Hireon does not sell personal information in the traditional sense of that term. To make a request regarding your data, contact us using the details below and we'll respond promptly.

10. Children's Privacy

Hireon's course listings and gift card offers are intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our site, course listings, or gift card offers evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, a course listing, or a gift card offer? Reach out any time — we're happy to explain further.
